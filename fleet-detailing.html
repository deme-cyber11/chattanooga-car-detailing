When You Need Fleet Detailing

A work truck covered in job-site mud. Service vans that smell like the last three jobs. Company cars that show up to client meetings looking like they haven't been washed since Q1. Your fleet is a rolling advertisement for your business. Customers and clients form impressions from the vehicles parked in their driveway or lot. Clean, maintained vehicles signal professionalism; neglected ones do the opposite. Fleet detailing isn't a luxury for most Chattanooga businesses — it's maintenance, the same way you wouldn't skip oil changes. And unlike oil changes, we come to you.

How Fleet Detailing Works

  1. 1

    Fleet assessment

    We review your vehicle count, types, and current condition, then quote per vehicle based on size and service level needed. Recurring programs get discounted rates.

  2. 2

    Scheduling

    We set up a recurring schedule — monthly, quarterly, or as-needed — that fits your operation. Most businesses prefer off-hours service so vehicles are available during the day.

  3. 3

    On-site service

    We come to your facility, lot, or yard. You don't move vehicles anywhere. We work through the fleet systematically and minimize your downtime.

  4. 4

    Documentation

    We provide a completion report noting what was done on each vehicle and flagging any damage or issues observed during service.

What Fleet Detailing Includes

  • Exterior wash — hand wash or foam wash depending on vehicle type and condition
  • Interior cleaning — vacuum, wipe-down, odor treatment where needed
  • Wheel and tire cleaning
  • Window cleaning, interior and exterior
  • Paint protection applied on full exterior detail jobs
  • Damage observation and reporting
  • Written flat-price quote per vehicle before any work begins
  • Recurring program rates available for regular clients

What Does Fleet Detailing Cost?

Fleet pricing depends on vehicle count, vehicle type, and service level. Standard exterior wash programs for commercial vans start in the $40–$80 per vehicle range. Full detail programs (interior + exterior) run $150–$350 per vehicle depending on size and condition. Recurring program clients receive discounted per-vehicle rates. Call (423) 589-1682 for a fleet-specific quote — we'll come to your location for the assessment.

How does fleet pricing work?

We quote per vehicle for one-time service or set up a recurring monthly or quarterly program at a discounted rate for regular clients. Fleet size, vehicle type, and service level all factor in. Call for a fleet quote — we'll assess your vehicles on-site.

Can you detail our vehicles at our facility?

Yes — we come to your location. Whether that's a business parking lot, warehouse, or vehicle yard, we bring all equipment and supplies. No need to drop vehicles off anywhere.

What services are available for fleet vehicles?

Exterior wash, full exterior detail, interior cleaning, odor treatment, and paint protection. Ceramic coating is available for vehicles where long-term paint protection is a priority. We tailor the service level to your needs and budget.
