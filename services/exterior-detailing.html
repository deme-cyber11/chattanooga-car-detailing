When You Need an Exterior Detail

Swirl marks visible in direct sunlight. Paint that looks dull even right after washing. Water spots that reappear within hours. A hood or roof with visible oxidation. These are clear coat problems that a standard car wash doesn't address. Chattanooga's spring pollen season makes this worse than most markets — pollen bonds to clear coat and bakes in under the Tennessee sun. A wash removes loose pollen. Clay bar treatment removes the embedded layer that's actually degrading your paint. That's why clay bar is a standard step in every exterior detail we do here — skipping it means any wax or sealant you apply is sitting on a contaminated surface.

How Exterior Detailing Works

Step 1 — Two-bucket hand wash: We wash with two buckets — one clean soap, one rinse — so grit never goes back on the paint. Wheels and wheel wells get separate brushes and degreasers.

Step 2 — Clay bar decontamination: A clay bar is worked across every panel with lubricant. It pulls bonded contamination — pollen, industrial fallout, tree sap, overspray — from the paint. The surface goes from rough to glass-smooth.

Step 3 — Machine polish: A polisher with appropriate pad removes swirl marks and light oxidation, restoring depth and gloss. Heavier defects require a more aggressive approach — we assess and tell you what's realistic before starting.

Step 4 — Wax or paint sealant: A quality wax or synthetic sealant goes on the freshly prepared surface. Sealants last 4–6 months in Chattanooga's humid climate. Ceramic coating is available as a longer-term option.

What Every Exterior Detail Includes

  • Two-bucket hand wash — safe for all paint types
  • Wheel and tire cleaning, tire dressing
  • Clay bar decontamination — every panel
  • Machine polish for swirl marks and light oxidation
  • Wax or paint sealant application

Full exterior detail runs $150–$300 in Chattanooga. Compact vehicles start around $150; full-size trucks and SUVs with heavier oxidation push toward $300. Paint correction (for more significant defects) starts at $300 as a separate service. The estimate is free — see our full pricing guide.

What's the difference between a car wash and exterior detailing?

A car wash removes surface dirt with water and soap. Exterior detailing includes clay bar decontamination, machine polishing, and a protective layer that lasts months. The result is dramatically better and stays that way far longer.

How long does it take?

A standard exterior detail takes 2–4 hours depending on vehicle size and paint condition. Heavier correction work takes longer. We give you an honest time estimate at booking.

Is clay bar safe for my paint?

Yes — done with proper lubricant, it's safe on all paint types including clear coat and matte finishes. It removes bonded contamination that washing can't reach. It's a standard, non-aggressive step in professional exterior detailing.
