More Than a Wash — A True Exterior Restoration

Chattanooga vehicles face a lot. Spring pollen blankets every surface from February through May. Railroad brake dust and industrial fallout from the Tennessee River Valley embeds iron particles in paint that cause rust spots if untreated. UV exposure at Tennessee's latitude fades and oxidizes clear coats year-round. And the oak canopy that makes Chattanooga beautiful deposits sap and tannins on everything parked beneath it.

A car wash removes surface dirt. Exterior detailing removes the contamination embedded in your paint, corrects the swirls and scratches in your clear coat, and applies protection that actually lasts. The difference between the two is the difference between a clean car and a truly restored vehicle.

Our Exterior Detailing Process

  1. Pre-rinse & wheel cleaning: Wheels and wheel wells are cleaned first (the dirtiest parts of any car) before the paint is touched. Brake dust and iron deposits are dissolved with a dedicated wheel cleaner.
  2. pH-neutral hand wash: Two-bucket wash method with pH-neutral shampoo prevents swirl introduction during washing. Door jambs, trim, and lower body panels get extra attention.
  3. Clay bar decontamination: A professional clay bar removes industrial fallout, embedded rail dust, tree sap, and overspray that bonded to your paint during normal use. This step is essential — nothing polishes properly over contaminated paint.
  4. Machine polish: Depending on paint condition, one or more stages of machine polishing remove swirl marks, light scratches, oxidation, and water spots from the clear coat. This is where dull, swirled paint becomes glass-smooth.
  5. Wax, sealant, or coating: A quality carnauba wax, synthetic paint sealant, or professional coating is applied to protect and enhance the freshly corrected paint.
  6. Glass, trim & tires: All exterior glass is cleaned, trim is dressed with a UV-protective product, and tires are finished with a non-sling tire dressing.

Paint Correction Explained

Paint correction is the process of removing surface defects from your clear coat using machine polishers and abrasive compounds. Most vehicles have swirl marks from improper washing, light scratches, and oxidation from UV exposure. A one-stage correction removes the majority of these defects; a two or three-stage correction achieves a near-flawless finish often described as "mirror-like." Paint correction is permanent — those swirls are gone, not hidden.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a car wash and exterior detailing?

A car wash removes surface dirt. Exterior detailing is a multi-step process: hand wash, clay bar decontamination, machine polishing to remove swirls and oxidation, and protective wax or sealant. Detailing restores and protects paint — a car wash just cleans the surface.

How much does exterior detailing cost in Chattanooga?

Basic exterior detail (hand wash, clay, wax) runs $100–$180. Full detail with paint correction starts at $200–$350 and can reach $600+ for multi-stage correction. Vehicle size affects pricing.

How often should I get my car detailed in Chattanooga?

Most Chattanooga vehicles benefit from a full exterior detail every 3–6 months. Tennessee's pollen, industrial fallout, and UV exposure all degrade paint rapidly. Monthly washes and quarterly clay bar decontamination between full details keep paint in top condition.
