What a Real Interior Detail Looks Like

Most Chattanooga drivers have sat in a car that's been through a quick interior "detail" — a surface vacuum, some ArmorAll on the dash, and a pine tree air freshener. That's not detailing. A true interior detail is a methodical, surface-by-surface deep clean that leaves no corner untouched: under the seats, in every cup holder crevice, in every door pocket, and between every seat seam.

At Peak Shine Detailing (Scenic City Detailing), we approach interior detailing the same way a restoration shop approaches a classic car. Every surface material — leather, vinyl, fabric, plastic, glass — gets the correct product and process. Nothing gets masked or skipped.

What's Included in Our Interior Detail

  • Deep vacuum: All seating surfaces, carpets, floor mats, trunk, and hard-to-reach areas including under seats and between cushions. We use compressed air to blow debris from crevices before vacuuming.
  • Steam cleaning: High-temperature steam sanitizes and deep-cleans upholstery, floor mats, door panels, and hard plastic surfaces. Steam kills bacteria, dust mites, and mold without chemicals — critical in Chattanooga's humid climate.
  • Leather conditioning: Leather seats and surfaces are cleaned with a pH-balanced cleaner then conditioned with a professional leather conditioner that restores moisture and prevents cracking and fading.
  • Plastic and vinyl treatment: All interior plastics and vinyl are cleaned and dressed with a non-greasy protectant that restores color and provides UV protection.
  • Interior glass cleaning: Dashboard glass and all interior windows are cleaned with streak-free glass cleaner for perfect visibility.
  • Odor elimination: We treat odors at the source with enzymatic cleaners rather than masking with fragrances. For severe odors, ozone treatment is available as an add-on.
  • Detail brushwork: Vents, buttons, trim gaps, and all tight spaces are cleaned with dedicated detail brushes.

Why Chattanooga's Climate Demands More

The Tennessee Valley's heat and humidity are hard on car interiors. Mold and mildew can establish in carpet and fabric upholstery when moisture gets trapped — especially in vehicles that sit with windows closed in the summer heat. Our steam cleaning process sanitizes as it cleans, addressing mold and bacteria that a surface vacuum misses entirely. Chattanooga's abundant tree canopy also means pollen, sap, and organic debris get tracked in regularly — another reason a true deep clean matters here more than in drier climates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does interior detailing take in Chattanooga?

A standard interior detail takes 2–4 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. Heavily soiled interiors, pet hair, or odor treatment may take 4–6 hours. We don't rush — every surface gets full attention.

How much does interior detailing cost in Chattanooga?

Interior detailing typically runs $150–$300 for sedans and compact SUVs. Trucks, full-size SUVs, and heavily soiled vehicles run $200–$350. Odor and pet hair treatments may add $50–$100. All prices quoted upfront.

Can interior detailing remove pet hair and odors?

Yes — both are core parts of our deep interior detail. We use specialized pet hair tools, commercial steam cleaning, and enzymatic odor treatments that neutralize odor at the source. Severe smoke or pet odors may benefit from optional ozone treatment.
