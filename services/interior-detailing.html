Interior detailing is a deep, surface-by-surface process: vacuum, compressed air, steam cleaning, leather conditioning, odor treatment at the source. We come to your home or office. Flat quote before we start.

When You Need an Interior Detail

Dog hair worked into seat fabric. A coffee smell that's been there long enough you stopped noticing it. Sticky cup holders. A dash faded and grimy from years of neglect. Most drivers put up with a degraded interior because they're busy — and a "quick clean" from a drive-through wash doesn't actually fix anything. A real interior detail is methodical: every surface, every crevice, the right product for each material. Done right, it restores what was there before the daily grind set in. Left too long, upholstery fabric degrades, leather cracks, and embedded odors become permanent.

How Interior Detailing Works

Step 1 — Removal and pre-treatment: All floor mats out. Loose debris cleared. Compressed air blown through every vent, seam, and crevice to push trapped dirt into reachable areas.

Step 2 — Deep vacuum: Every seat surface, carpet, mat, trunk, and tight space vacuumed thoroughly. Under the seats, between the cushions, into every door pocket.

Step 3 — Steam cleaning: High-temperature steam sanitizes and deep-cleans upholstery, carpet, door panels, and hard plastic surfaces. Steam kills bacteria and mold without harsh chemicals — critical in Chattanooga's humid climate where mold can establish in fabric faster than in drier regions.

Step 4 — Surface wipe-down and conditioning: Every hard surface cleaned — dash, console, door panels, cup holders. Leather cleaned and conditioned. Vinyl dressed with a non-greasy protectant.

Step 5 — Glass and final inspection: Interior glass cleaned streak-free. Odors treated at the source with enzymatic cleaners. Final inspection before we hand the car back.

What Every Interior Detail Includes

  • Deep vacuum — seats, carpet, mats, trunk, all crevices with compressed air pre-treatment
  • Steam cleaning of upholstery, carpet, and hard surfaces
  • Leather and vinyl cleaning + conditioning
  • All hard surface wipe-down: dash, console, doors, cup holders
  • Interior glass cleaning, streak-free
  • Enzymatic odor treatment — not fragrance masking
  • Written flat-price quote before work begins
  • Full cleanup and final inspection

What Does Interior Detailing Cost?

Interior detailing in Chattanooga runs $100–$200 for most vehicles. Compact sedans start around $100. Full-size SUVs, minivans, and trucks run toward $200. Heavier soiling, significant pet hair, or ozone odor treatment add to the total — we'll tell you upfront. The estimate is free. See our full pricing guide.

How long does an interior detail take?

A standard interior detail takes 2–4 hours for a clean-to-moderate vehicle. Heavily soiled interiors, pet hair, or vehicles needing odor treatment run 4–6 hours. We give you a time window at booking.

Can interior detailing remove pet hair and odors?

Yes — both are core parts of what we do. Specialized pet hair tools, steam extraction, and enzymatic treatments handle both. For severe smoke or pet odors, ozone treatment is available as an add-on — it neutralizes at a molecular level rather than masking.

Do I need to be present?

No. We just need access to the vehicle. Most customers leave it with us and return when done. We text when we wrap up.
