Chattanooga Ceramic Coating — Semi-Permanent Paint Protection

Ceramic coating bonds to your clear coat and protects paint from pollen, UV, humidity, and environmental contamination for years — not weeks. Flat quote before we start. We come to you.

When You Need Ceramic Coating

Wax works. For a few weeks. Then Chattanooga's humidity, intense spring pollen season, and daily UV exposure break it down, and you're back to unprotected clear coat. If you're applying wax twice a year and still watching your paint degrade, ceramic coating is the logical upgrade. Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds to the clear coat, creating a semi-permanent hydrophobic layer. Water beads and rolls off. Pollen and contaminants don't bond the way they do on bare paint. UV protection is built in. The result is a finish that requires dramatically less maintenance and actually holds up to Chattanooga's climate year-round.

How Ceramic Coating Works

Step 1 — Paint assessment: We inspect the paint under proper lighting to identify swirl marks, water spots, oxidation, and scratches. Ceramic coating locks in whatever condition the paint is in — so we tell you honestly whether correction is needed before application.

Step 2 — Paint correction (if needed): Any significant defects are addressed before coating. This is a separate step, quoted separately, and only done if your paint genuinely needs it. We won't sell correction work you don't need.

Step 3 — Surface preparation: The paint is cleaned, decontaminated with a clay bar, and wiped down with an IPA (isopropyl alcohol) solution to remove all polish residue and oils. The surface must be chemically clean before coating application.

Step 4 — Coating application: The ceramic coating is applied panel by panel with an applicator block and leveling cloth. Flash time and ambient conditions are monitored throughout. Application technique matters significantly — this is where professional application earns its cost.

Step 5 — Cure and inspection: The coating requires time to cure. We'll give you maintenance instructions: what to avoid in the first week, how to wash properly going forward, and what to expect from the coating's performance.

What Every Ceramic Coating Job Includes

  • Paint assessment and honest recommendation before quoting
  • Full decontamination wash and clay bar treatment
  • IPA wipe-down for chemical surface preparation
  • Professional coating application — all painted panels
  • Post-application inspection
  • Written aftercare instructions
  • Written flat-price quote before any work begins

What Does Ceramic Coating Cost?

Ceramic coating in Chattanooga runs $500–$1,800 depending on the product tier, vehicle size, and whether paint correction is required before application. Entry-level coatings in the 1–2 year range start around $500 for a standard sedan. Multi-year coatings with longer durability and better chemical resistance run $800–$1,800. The estimate is free and includes an honest paint assessment — see our full pricing guide.

How long does ceramic coating last?

Professionally applied coatings last 2–5 years depending on the product tier and maintenance. Chattanooga's humidity and pollen make a proper coating significantly more effective than wax, which breaks down in weeks under these conditions.

Does my car need paint correction first?

In most cases, yes. Ceramic coating seals in whatever the paint looks like at application — including swirl marks and oxidation. We assess at the vehicle and tell you honestly what prep is needed. If your paint is in good shape, you may not need correction at all.

Can I wash the car normally after coating?

Yes — and it's easier. The hydrophobic surface causes water and contaminants to bead and roll off rather than bonding. Hand wash with pH-neutral soap or a touchless automated wash. Avoid brush-type automated washes and harsh chemical cleaners.
