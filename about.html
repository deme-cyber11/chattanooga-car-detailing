About Us — Chattanooga Car Detailing Specialists

Locally operated. Honest pricing. We come to you.

Most Chattanooga drivers looking for a detailer have had the same experience: vague pricing until you show up, rushed work, or a "detail" that's really just a glorified car wash. We built Peak Shine Detailing around a different idea — show up on time, do the work right, and charge exactly what we quoted. We're not the cheapest option in the valley. We are the option that actually fixes the problem, whether that's embedded spring pollen, a mildewed cabin, or paint that hasn't seen clay bar since the dealership.

What We Do

We specialize in mobile car detailing for Chattanooga, Cleveland, Dalton, Fort Oglethorpe, Ringgold, and surrounding Southeast Tennessee and North Georgia communities. Our services cover everything from a basic exterior wash to full paint correction and ceramic coating — all done at your home or office, on your schedule. See our full services list or jump straight to pricing.

What Makes Us Different

Flat quotes before we touch anything. You get a written price before work starts. If we find something unexpected — heavier oxidation, more embedded contamination than expected — we stop and show you before any additional work proceeds. The price we quote is the price you pay.

Products chosen for this climate. Chattanooga's spring pollen season is aggressive. High humidity means mold and mildew establish in upholstery faster than in drier climates. We use clay bar treatment on every exterior detail because in this market, bonded pollen and industrial fallout are the norm — not the exception. Interior steam cleaning addresses mold and bacteria that a surface vacuum doesn't touch.

We call back the same day. No voicemail black holes. When you call (423) 589-1682, you reach someone who can actually schedule you — usually within the week. If we're full, we tell you that too.

Who We Serve

We serve homeowners and businesses across Chattanooga and the surrounding region — including Cleveland, Dalton, Fort Oglethorpe, Ringgold, and the Tennessee Valley communities in between. We travel to your location. If you're on the outer edge of our area, call us — we may still be able to help.

Our Commitment

Every job gets a written quote before any work starts. We don't disappear mid-job. We clean up after ourselves. And if something isn't right, we make it right before we leave — not after a follow-up call and a rescheduled appointment.
