Our Mobile Car Detailing Services in Chattanooga

Exterior Wash & Wax

Ideal for removing the heavy pollen and dust of Chattanooga's spring and summer, this service protects your paint from strong sun and frequent rain.

Interior Detailing

From mud tracked in after a hike to pet hair from family outings, our deep cleaning restores the cabin, fights humidity-related odors, and keeps the interior fresh.

Paint Correction

Over time, local road conditions and tree sap cause swirl marks and minor imperfections. Our paint correction restores depth and clarity to the finish.

Ceramic Coating

A ceramic coating protects against UV rays, tree sap, and road salt from occasional winter weather, and it keeps your vehicle looking its best with less effort, which suits busy Chattanooga drivers.

Frequently Asked Questions About Car Detailing Costs in Chattanooga

How much does mobile car detailing cost in Chattanooga?

The cost depends on your vehicle's size and current condition. Our pricing is competitive for the Chattanooga market and reflects the convenience and quality of having the work done in your driveway. Contact us for a personalized quote based on your vehicle and what it needs.

How long does mobile car detailing take?

For a standard car or mid-size SUV, a full mobile detail usually takes 2 to 4 hours, depending on the package and the vehicle's condition. More extensive services such as paint correction or ceramic coating take longer. We work efficiently without cutting corners, so your day is barely disrupted.

Do you serve all of Chattanooga?

Yes. Peak Shine Detailing serves neighborhoods throughout Chattanooga and the surrounding communities, and we bring mobile car detailing directly to you wherever you live in our service area.

How do Chattanooga's humidity and tree sap affect my car's finish?

Chattanooga's humid climate encourages mildew and odors inside the car, and tree sap from the area's abundant foliage can etch paint if it is not removed promptly. Our detailing services are designed for these local conditions, using the right products to remove sap safely and deep clean interiors so your vehicle is protected from environmental wear.
