How Much Does Car Detailing Cost in Chattanooga?

If you're searching for car detailing in Chattanooga, one of the first questions you probably have is: how much is this going to cost? The answer depends on what services you need, the size of your vehicle, and the condition it's in.

Here's a realistic breakdown of what Chattanooga car owners can expect to pay in 2025.

Basic Exterior Wash: $25–$50

A basic hand wash covers a thorough rinse, soap wash, wheel cleaning, and dry. This is maintenance-level cleaning — it won't remove embedded contaminants or restore faded paint, but it keeps your car looking presentable between details.

Interior Detailing: $149–$300

A proper interior detail goes far beyond vacuuming. Expect deep extraction of carpets and upholstery, leather conditioning, steam cleaning of vents and crevices, and thorough wipe-down of all surfaces. Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks will be on the higher end.

Exterior Detailing: $129–$275

This includes a hand wash, clay bar decontamination to remove bonded contaminants, machine polish for light imperfections, and a sealant or wax layer for protection. It's what most people think of when they hear "detailing."

Full Detail (Interior + Exterior): $250–$500

Combining interior and exterior services into one appointment is the most popular option. You'll get a complete transformation — inside and out — typically in 3–5 hours depending on vehicle size and condition.

Ceramic Coating: $599–$1,500+

Ceramic coating is a long-term investment. The coating itself bonds to your paint and provides hydrophobic protection for 2–5 years. The price includes paint preparation (wash, decontamination, and often a single-stage polish) plus the coating application. Higher-end packages include multi-layer coatings and longer warranties.

Paint Correction: $349–$800+

If your paint has swirl marks, scratches, or oxidation, paint correction uses machine compounding and polishing to restore clarity. A single-stage correction handles light defects, while a multi-stage correction addresses deeper damage. This is often done before ceramic coating for the best results.

What Affects the Price?

Several factors influence your final cost:

  • Vehicle size — A compact sedan costs less than a full-size truck or SUV
  • Condition — Heavy pet hair, stains, or neglected paint requires more labor
  • Service type — Basic maintenance vs. full restoration-level work
  • Mobile vs. shop — Mobile detailing may carry a small convenience fee
