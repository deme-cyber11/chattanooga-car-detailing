What Mobile Car Detailing Costs in Chattanooga

Chattanooga drivers value the convenience of mobile car detailing. Many homes in the foothills around the city, from established properties to newer developments, have small driveways or limited space, which makes washing a car at home difficult. Humid weather, frequent rain, and red clay soil mean vehicles collect grime and stubborn stains quickly, and mature trees add pollen, sap, and leaf debris. For busy families and commuters, finding time for a car wash is a challenge, and protecting a vehicle from intense summer sun and red clay is a priority. Mobile detailing solves both problems by coming to your door.

Our Mobile Car Detailing Services and What They Cost

Exterior Wash & Wax

Our exterior service removes the pollen, tree sap, and red clay dust that build up on Chattanooga vehicles. We use techniques and products that lift these contaminants safely, restore the shine, and protect the paint from strong sun and frequent rain.

Interior Deep Clean

Chattanooga's humidity and active, outdoor lifestyles take a toll on interiors through moisture, pet hair, and everyday grime. Our deep clean removes odors, thoroughly cleans upholstery and carpets, and sanitizes surfaces for a fresh, healthy cabin.

Paint Correction

Narrow, tree-lined roads and occasional gravel patches leave swirl marks and light scratches. Paint correction removes these imperfections, restores clarity and depth to the finish, and prepares it for protection against strong UV rays.

Ceramic Coating Application

For the strongest protection against acid rain, tree sap, UV damage, and red clay, a ceramic coating forms a durable, long-lasting barrier. It makes future cleaning easier, resists stains, enhances gloss, and keeps your car looking its best year-round.

Frequently Asked Questions About Mobile Car Detailing Costs in Chattanooga

How much does mobile car detailing cost in Chattanooga?

The cost depends on vehicle size and the service package you choose. SUVs and larger family vehicles take more time, and red clay and pollen add work. Prices generally range from $40 to $80 for a basic wash to $200 to $400 for a full detail, with ceramic coating.

How long does mobile car detailing take?

A standard mobile detail on a typical vehicle usually takes 2 to 3 hours. Vehicles often need extra attention because of red clay dust, tree sap, and pollen. More intensive services such as paint correction or ceramic coating can take 4 to 6 hours, and we work efficiently so your schedule is not disrupted.

Do you serve all of Chattanooga?

Yes. Peak Shine Detailing serves neighborhoods throughout Chattanooga and the surrounding communities. Our mobile units are equipped to reach your home or workplace anywhere in our service area.

How do you handle the red clay stains common in Chattanooga?

Red clay is one of the biggest challenges for vehicles here, and we treat it carefully. Our process uses clay bar treatment, iron removers, and gentle but effective wheel cleaners to lift red clay deposits from paint, wheels, and undercarriages without damaging the finish. Thorough removal prevents long-term staining.
