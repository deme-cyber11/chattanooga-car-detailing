How Much Does Car Detailing Cost in Chattanooga, TN? [2025]

A straightforward pricing breakdown for every level of detailing — from a basic interior clean to ceramic coating installation.

Home › Blog › Car Detailing Cost Chattanooga

Chattanooga drivers often ask what car detailing costs — and the honest answer is that it ranges from $50 for a basic exterior wash and wax to $2,000+ for a full ceramic coating package. The right service depends on what your vehicle needs and what you want to achieve. Here's a complete breakdown of 2025 detailing prices in the Chattanooga area.

Interior Detailing Prices in Chattanooga

Interior detailing involves deep cleaning all cabin surfaces — vacuuming, steam cleaning, leather conditioning, odor elimination, and detailing all plastics and glass.

  • Basic interior clean (vacuum + wipe down): $50–$100
  • Standard interior detail: $120–$200 (sedan/compact)
  • Full interior detail with steam cleaning: $150–$300
  • Trucks and large SUVs: Add $30–$75 to above prices
  • Pet hair removal add-on: $50–$100
  • Odor treatment (ozone): $75–$150

Exterior Detailing Prices in Chattanooga

Exterior detailing ranges from a basic hand wash and wax to full multi-stage paint correction that removes swirls and scratches from your clear coat.

  • Hand wash + wax (no correction): $75–$150
  • Exterior detail with clay bar + wax: $100–$180
  • Full exterior with single-stage paint correction: $200–$350
  • Multi-stage paint correction: $350–$600+

Full Detail (Interior + Exterior) Prices

Most clients booking a full detail on a sedan or midsize SUV spend $175–$400 depending on condition. A heavily soiled vehicle with paint correction needs can reach $500–$700. Most detailers in Chattanooga offer a bundled rate that saves you 10–15% vs. booking interior and exterior separately.

Ceramic Coating Prices in Chattanooga

Ceramic coating is a semi-permanent paint protection product that lasts 2–9 years. It's a bigger investment than a wax job, but the long-term protection and gloss enhancement — especially in Tennessee's high-pollen, high-UV environment — makes it a popular choice for anyone planning to keep their vehicle.

  • Entry-level coating (2–3 year): $500–$800
  • Mid-tier coating (4–5 year): $800–$1,400
  • Premium coating (7–9 year): $1,400–$2,000+

Note: All ceramic coating packages include mandatory paint correction before application.

What Affects Car Detailing Prices in Chattanooga?

  • Vehicle size: Trucks and full-size SUVs cost more than compact sedans — more surface area, more time.
  • Condition: A heavily soiled interior or oxidized paint with significant swirl damage takes more time and product.
  • Service type: Basic washes are cheap; paint correction and coating installations require significant skill and time.
  • Detailer quality: Professional shops with trained technicians charge more than mobile detailers with a bucket — but the results are dramatically different.

Is Car Detailing Worth It in Chattanooga?

Tennessee's environment is particularly hard on vehicles — pollen season, summer UV, tree sap, and humidity all accelerate paint degradation. Regular detailing maintains your vehicle's value, makes daily maintenance easier, and extends the life of your paint finish. A well-maintained vehicle can retain thousands more in resale value than one that's been neglected. Most Chattanooga drivers who try professional detailing once become regulars.
