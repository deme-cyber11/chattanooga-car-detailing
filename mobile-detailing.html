Chattanooga Mobile Car Detailing — We Come to You

Professional car detailing at your home or workplace. We bring everything — water, equipment, and products. You get your time back. Flat quote before we start.

When You Need Mobile Detailing

Driving a dirty car to a detail shop, waiting an hour, driving back — and repeating that twice a year — costs more time than most people realize. Mobile car detailing flips the model. We come to your home driveway or office parking lot, do the work while you're already there, and hand you back a clean car without you moving. This works especially well in Chattanooga's spread-out geography: whether you're in Hixson, East Brainerd, Ooltewah, or Signal Mountain, the drive to a shop adds up. Mobile service eliminates it entirely.

How Mobile Detailing Works

Step 1 — Book and confirm: Call or text (423) 589-1682. Tell us your vehicle size, what you need, and your location. We confirm a date and time window — typically within the week.

Step 2 — We show up fully equipped: Our mobile unit brings professional-grade products, equipment, and water. You don't need to provide a thing except access to the vehicle.

Step 3 — We do the work: Full interior, exterior, or both — same service and same products as any fixed-location detail shop. We don't cut corners because we're at your driveway.

Step 4 — Inspection and handoff: We walk you through the vehicle, show you what we did, and answer any questions. Payment due at completion.

What Mobile Detailing Includes

  • We bring our own water, equipment, and all professional products
  • Same service quality as a fixed-location detail — no shortcuts for mobile jobs
  • Available at your home driveway, workplace, apartment complex, or any accessible location
  • Interior detail, exterior detail, full detail, paint correction, and ceramic coating all available as mobile services
  • No mobile surcharge — same pricing as standard service rates
  • Written flat-price quote before work begins

What Does Mobile Detailing Cost?

Mobile detailing is priced the same as our standard services — there's no extra fee for coming to you. Interior detail runs $100–$200. Full detail (interior + exterior) runs $200–$400. See our full pricing guide for all service ranges. The estimate is free.

Do you bring your own water?

Yes — we're fully self-contained. We bring our own water supply, so we don't need access to your hose or outdoor spigot (though we can use it if available and you prefer).

Where can you detail my car?

We come to your home driveway, workplace parking lot, or any location where the vehicle can be safely accessed. We serve Chattanooga and surrounding communities including Cleveland, Dalton, Fort Oglethorpe, Ringgold, and the Tennessee Valley suburbs. Check our service areas.

Do I need to be present during the detail?

No. We just need access to the vehicle. Most customers leave the car and go about their day. We text you when we're finished.

Ready to Book Your Mobile Detail?

Most vehicles scheduled within the week. We come to you — no drop-off, no wait, no wasted drive.
