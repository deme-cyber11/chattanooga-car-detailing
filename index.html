How It Works

What a Professional Detail
Actually Looks Like

From the booking call to the final walkthrough — here's what separates a real detail from a basic wash.

1

Booking Call — Vehicle Assessment

We confirm your vehicle, your goals, and your schedule. You tell us what the main issues are; we tell you honestly what services make sense.

2

Pre-Detail Inspection

We document existing damage — scratches, dents, stains — before starting. Paint condition is assessed if correction is in scope. No surprises at the end.

3

Interior Work First

Vacuum, compressed air blow-out, surface wipe-down, glass cleaning, leather conditioning. Every crevice, not just eye-level surfaces.

4

Exterior Decontamination & Polish

Hand wash, iron decontamination, clay bar, polishing (if in scope), and sealant or wax application. Paint looks better than it has in years.

5

Final Walkthrough With You

We walk the vehicle with you before calling it done. If anything isn't right, we address it on the spot. Results guaranteed or we come back.
