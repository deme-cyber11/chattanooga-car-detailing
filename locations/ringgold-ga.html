Your Car Looks Like It's Been Lived In. Because It Has.

Ringgold sits right on the Tennessee-Georgia state line, just a few miles south of Chattanooga on I-75 — Catoosa County's residential character with heavy I-75 commute exposure. Vehicles in Ringgold accumulate road grime from the interstate corridor, Georgia red clay from unpaved residential roads and rural edges of town, and the same spring pollen season that hammers the Chattanooga market a few miles north. Finding a quality mobile detailer that will actually cross the state line is harder than it should be. Homeowners near Tiger Creek, along Nashville Street, and in the newer subdivisions off Boynton Drive deal with red clay tracked onto paint and into interiors every time it rains. We come to Ringgold regularly — same service, same pricing, same flat quote before we start.

What Skipping the Detail Is Costing You

  • Georgia red clay is relentless. Red clay from Catoosa County's roads bonds to paint and requires clay bar treatment to fully remove. Standard washing removes the surface layer and leaves the bonded clay degrading your clear coat.
  • Interstate grime accumulates fast. Daily I-75 commutes to Chattanooga deposit road tar, brake dust, and exhaust particulate on paint and wheels. Decontamination washing addresses what a standard rinse leaves behind.
  • Interior wear on commuter vehicles. Long commutes mean vehicles accumulate coffee, food, and daily passenger wear faster than short-trip vehicles. Steam extraction and deep cleaning reverse what builds up over months.
  • Resale value. A clean vehicle at private sale or trade-in commands more money. A detail before listing is almost always positive ROI.

What We Actually Do

Exterior: Two-bucket hand wash, clay bar decontamination for bonded Georgia red clay and I-75 road contaminants, machine polish for swirl marks, wax or paint sealant for lasting protection. Wheels and tires cleaned and dressed separately.

Interior: Full vacuum, compressed air, steam cleaning, surface wipe-down, leather/vinyl conditioning, interior glass, enzymatic odor treatment.

Mobile: We cross the state line. We come to your Ringgold home or workplace — no trip up to Chattanooga required.
