Auto Detailing in Fort Oglethorpe, Georgia

Fort Oglethorpe is a city of approximately 10,000 residents spanning Catoosa and Walker counties in Northwest Georgia, best known as the home of the Chickamauga and Chattanooga National Military Park — the first federally designated military park in the United States. Along Battlefield Parkway and throughout the surrounding neighborhoods, Fort Oglethorpe residents depend on their vehicles for daily commutes into Chattanooga and beyond. Peak Shine Detailing brings licensed, insured, certified auto detailing professionals to Fort Oglethorpe, protecting your paint and interior from red Georgia clay, highway debris, and relentless Southern sun.

Our Auto Detailing Process

Every vehicle that comes through Peak Shine Detailing follows our proven 5-step process, designed to restore and protect your car inside and out.

Step 1: Vehicle Assessment & Consultation

We start with a thorough walk-around inspection of your vehicle, documenting existing damage, paint condition, and interior wear. We discuss your goals and recommend the right package for your needs and budget.

Step 2: Exterior Wash & Decontamination

Using pH-neutral shampoos and a two-bucket wash method, we safely remove dirt, grime, and contaminants. Clay bar treatment lifts embedded particles that regular washing misses, leaving a perfectly smooth surface.

Step 3: Paint Correction & Protection

Machine polishing removes swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation. We then apply high-grade sealants or ceramic coatings to lock in that showroom shine and protect against Georgia weather and UV damage.

Step 4: Interior Deep Clean & Conditioning

Steam cleaning, hot water extraction, and specialized brushes tackle every surface — seats, carpets, dash, door panels, and crevices. Leather gets conditioned, plastics are UV-protected, and odors are eliminated at the source.

Step 5: Final Inspection & Detail Walkthrough

We perform a comprehensive quality check under LED inspection lights, then walk you through every detail. You don't pay until you're 100% satisfied with the results.
