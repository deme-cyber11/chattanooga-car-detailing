Your Car Looks Like It's Been Lived In. Because It Has.

Fort Oglethorpe sits in Catoosa County at the intersection of Battlefield Parkway and US-27 — suburban residential mixed with busy commercial corridors and easy access to the Chickamauga Battlefield and surrounding natural areas. Vehicles here commute into Chattanooga on US-27 daily, accumulating road grime, brake dust, and industrial particulate from the corridor. Spring pollen from northwest Georgia's tree-heavy landscape settles on paint surfaces through April and May. And like much of Catoosa County, red clay from surrounding roads and yards makes its way into vehicle interiors and onto paint. Residents along LaFayette Road, near the Battlefield Parkway shopping corridors, and in the growing subdivisions off Cloud Springs Road know that red clay dust coats everything within hours of a rain. Finding a mobile detailer willing to come to Fort Oglethorpe is harder than it should be. We come regularly — same service, same pricing, same flat quote.

What Skipping the Detail Is Costing You

  • Red clay and Georgia road grime. Catoosa County's clay-heavy soils mean vehicles accumulate clay contamination on paint that requires clay bar treatment to fully remove — a standard wash leaves the bonded layer.
  • Battlefield and outdoor activity debris. Trips to Chickamauga Battlefield or outdoor recreational areas mean organic debris, trail dust, and pollen get tracked into vehicle interiors. Steam extraction addresses what a vacuum misses.
  • US-27 commute wear. The daily run to Chattanooga deposits brake dust and road tar on wheels and paint surfaces. Decontamination washing addresses this at the source.
  • Resale value. A clean vehicle at private sale or trade-in commands more money. A detail before listing is almost always positive ROI.

What We Actually Do

Exterior: Two-bucket hand wash, clay bar decontamination for Georgia clay and road fallout, machine polish for swirl marks, wax or paint sealant.

Interior: Full vacuum, compressed air, steam cleaning, surface wipe-down, leather/vinyl conditioning, interior glass, enzymatic odor treatment.

Mobile: We come to Fort Oglethorpe — no trip required.
