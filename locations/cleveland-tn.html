Your Car Looks Like It's Been Lived In. Because It Has.

Red clay worked into the carpet after a trip down a Bradley County back road. Dog hair from the seat to the seat back. Water spots on the hood from the last storm that reappear every time the morning sun hits. Swirl marks from the rotating brushes at that automated wash on Paul Huff Parkway — those brushes pick up grit from the car before yours and drag it across your paint at speed. A drive-through wash doesn't fix any of that. A real detail is different: by hand, with the right products for each surface, done at your home or office so you don't rearrange your day.

What Skipping the Detail Is Costing You

Paint oxidation builds

UV exposure and Cleveland's outdoor lifestyle wear down clear coat year over year. The longer swirl marks and contamination go untreated, the more correction work — and cost — it takes to restore.

Interior materials degrade

Leather and vinyl that aren't conditioned dry, crack, and fade. Fabric upholstery that isn't cleaned holds odors and allergens that get harder to extract the longer they're embedded.

Resale value drops

A vehicle that presents well — clean paint, clean interior — consistently commands more at private sale and trade-in. A detail before listing is almost always positive ROI.

Odors set in

Kids, pets, food — embedded smells get harder to remove the longer they've been in upholstery. Deep extraction removes what a vacuum swipe leaves behind.

What We Actually Do

Exterior

Hand wash (two-bucket method to keep grit off your paint), clay bar to remove bonded contamination, polish for swirl marks and light oxidation, wax or paint sealant for protection. Wheels and tires cleaned and dressed separately.

Interior

Full vacuum — seats, carpet, mats, trunk. Compressed air through vents and seams. Wipe-down of all surfaces: dash, doors, console, cup holders. Leather or vinyl conditioning. Glass cleaning, all interior surfaces. Steam cleaning for deep sanitization.

Mobile service

We come to your Cleveland home or office. You keep your time. We show up, do the work, and hand you back a car that looks like you bought it yesterday.
