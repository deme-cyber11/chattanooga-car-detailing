Your Car Looks Like It's Been Lived In. Because It Has.

Ooltewah is one of Chattanooga's fastest-growing communities — newer subdivisions, long I-75 commutes, families with active lifestyles. That translates to vehicles that accumulate road grime from the interstate, spring pollen from the surrounding valley, and daily passenger wear. The drive-through wash at the gas station handles the surface layer. It doesn't handle swirl marks from the rotating brushes, bonded pollen in the clear coat, pet hair in the rear seats, or the coffee smell that's been there since February. A real mobile detail does. By hand, with the right products, at your Ooltewah driveway while you're already home.

What Skipping the Detail Is Costing You

  • New development, dusty roads. Ooltewah's active construction and unpaved subdivision roads mean vehicles accumulate fine particulate that bonds to paint surfaces. Standard washes can't pull bonded contamination — clay bar treatment can.
  • Interstate commute grime. Long I-75 runs pick up road tar, brake dust, and exhaust particulate that build up on paint and wheels. This requires actual degreasing and decontamination, not a surface rinse.
  • Family wear adds up fast. Ooltewah's family-heavy demographic means car interiors take daily punishment — kids, sports gear, pets, food. The longer embedded odors and stains go untreated, the harder they are to remove.
  • Resale timing matters. With active home and vehicle markets in east Hamilton County, presenting a clean vehicle at sale time is straightforward ROI. A detail before listing is almost always worth more than it costs.

What We Actually Do

Exterior: Two-bucket hand wash, clay bar decontamination for bonded pollen and road fallout, machine polish for swirl marks, wax or paint sealant for real protection. Wheels and tires cleaned and dressed separately.

Interior: Full vacuum — seats, carpet, mats, trunk. Compressed air through vents and seams. Steam cleaning for deep sanitization. Surface wipe-down, leather and vinyl conditioning. Interior glass streak-free. Odor treatment at the source with enzymatic cleaners.

Mobile: We come to your Ooltewah home or office. You keep your time.
