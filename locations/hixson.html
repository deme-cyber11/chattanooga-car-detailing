Your Car Looks Like It's Been Lived In. Because It Has.

Tree sap from Hixson's heavy oak canopy. Water spots that reappear the minute the morning sun hits. Swirl marks from the automated wash on Highway 153 — those rotating brushes drag grit across your paint every pass. Dog hair embedded in the rear seat. A drive-through wash doesn't fix any of that. Hixson sits directly in the Tennessee River valley, which means pollen from spring through early summer is persistent and heavy. That pollen bonds to clear coat and bakes in. A standard wash removes the loose stuff and leaves the bonded layer degrading your paint. Clay bar treatment is the only fix, and it's a standard step in every exterior detail we do.

What Skipping the Detail Is Costing You

Paint oxidation compounds

UV exposure from open suburban roads and pollen that's bonded to the paint surface accelerate clear coat degradation. The longer it goes, the more correction work required later.

Interior odors embed

Hixson families know: sports bags, kids, pets, and Tennessee summer heat create cabin odors that surface cleaning doesn't reach. Steam extraction and enzymatic treatment are the only real fix.

Leather and vinyl crack

UV exposure through your windshield and side windows breaks down untreated leather and vinyl faster than most owners expect. Conditioning reverses early degradation and prevents advanced damage.

Resale value drops

Clean paint and a clean interior consistently command more money — whether you're trading in or selling privately. A detail is almost always positive ROI before a sale.

What We Actually Do

Exterior

Two-bucket hand wash, clay bar for bonded contamination, machine polish for swirl marks, wax or paint sealant for protection. Wheels and tires separately. Real paint protection — not a spray-and-wipe.

Interior

Full vacuum — every seat surface, carpet, mat, trunk. Compressed air through vents and seams. Steam cleaning for sanitization. Wipe-down of all surfaces. Leather and vinyl conditioning. Interior glass, streak-free. Odor treatment at the source, not masked.

Mobile

We come to your Hixson home or office. You don't rearrange your day. We handle it.
