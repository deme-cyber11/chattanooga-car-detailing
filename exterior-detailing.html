Exterior Detailing in Chattanooga

Professional exterior car detailing in Chattanooga. Hand wash, clay bar, polish, wax & sealant. Call (423) 589-1682.

Show-Ready Exterior Finish

Tennessee roads are tough on paint — road grime, pollen, tree sap, and UV exposure take their toll. Our exterior detailing service removes contaminants and restores that deep, glossy shine.

What's Included

  • Touchless pre-rinse and foam bath
  • Hand wash with pH-neutral shampoo
  • Clay bar decontamination
  • Single-stage machine polish
  • Sealant or carnauba wax protection
  • Tire and wheel deep clean
  • Trim restoration and tire dressing
  • Exterior glass treatment
